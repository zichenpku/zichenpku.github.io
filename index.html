Peking University Cancer Hospital · Wu Zhouqiao Research Group · April 2026–Present

Clinical and Multi-omic Study of Postoperative Intra-abdominal Infection after Gastrointestinal Cancer Surgery

Integrating clinical records with drainage-fluid proteomics, metabolomics, and 16S rRNA sequencing, alongside an independent peripheral-blood flow-cytometry cohort for systemic immune phenotyping. My work covers data cleaning, statistical modeling, differential and pathway analysis, cross-omic evidence synthesis, figure preparation, manuscript writing, local LLM deployment, and reproducible research workflows.

Current findings are exploratory associations and require prospective validation in a patient-matched cohort. Manuscript in preparation.

Shenda Hong Research Group · National Institute of Health Data Science at Peking University · September 2026 to Present

Short-term seizure forecasting from scalp EEG

Developing a scalp EEG pipeline for seizure warnings 5 to 10 minutes before onset, with patient-level data splits and beyond-chance evaluation. I have completed the study protocol, computing environment, code framework, and preliminary validation using the public CHB-MIT dataset.

The models performed better within individuals than across patients, while the permutation test was not statistically significant. Further validation using MORGOTH pretrained weights and HEEDB clinical data is pending. The project remains at the method-development stage and does not yet establish clinical utility.
