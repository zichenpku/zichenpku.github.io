Clinical and Multi-omic Study of Postoperative Intra-abdominal Infection after Gastrointestinal Cancer Surgery

Research focusIntegrating clinical records with drainage-fluid proteomics, metabolomics, and 16S rRNA sequencing to study postoperative infection. An independent peripheral-blood flow-cytometry cohort is used to assess systemic immune phenotypes.

My roleData cleaning, statistical modeling, differential and pathway analysis, cross-omics evidence synthesis, figure preparation, and manuscript writing. I also deploy local large language models (LLMs) and build reproducible research workflows.

Shenda Hong Research Group · National Institute of Health Data Science at Peking UniversitySeptember 2026 to Present

Short-term seizure forecasting from scalp EEG

Research focusDeveloping a scalp electroencephalography (EEG) pipeline for seizure warnings 5 to 10 minutes before onset, with patient-level data splits and evaluation against chance performance.

ProgressI have completed the study protocol, computing environment, code framework, and preliminary validation using the public CHB-MIT dataset. The models performed better within individuals than across patients, while the permutation test was not statistically significant.

Current statusFurther validation using MORGOTH pretrained weights and HEEDB clinical data is pending. The project remains at the method-development stage and does not yet establish clinical effectiveness.
